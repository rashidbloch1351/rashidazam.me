## 5. Canonicalization & Anti-Cannibalization Rules

1. **Self-Referential Canonicals**: Every indexable page MUST output a strict `<link rel="canonical" href="https://rashidazam.me/[route]" />`.
2. **No Duplicate Target Terms**: 
   - `/services/seo-services` owns "Technical SEO Services Pakistan" (commercial intent).
   - `/seo` owns "SEO Knowledge Hub" (informational/hub intent).
   - `/blog/seo-strategy-2026-rank-higher-google` owns "SEO Strategy 2026" (informational article).
3. **No Thin Overlapping Pages**: Merge any identical informational content into pillar topic pages or primary blog posts.
